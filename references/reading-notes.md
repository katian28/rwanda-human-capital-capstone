# Reading notes

## Hodler (2018)

### Central question

What were the short- and long-run effects of the 1994 genocide on Rwanda's economic output?

### Method

The paper uses synthetic control to construct a counterfactual Rwanda from a weighted donor pool of Sub-Saharan African countries. Neighboring countries are excluded because refugee flows and regional conflict could create spillovers. Inference relies on placebo studies and comparisons of pre- and post-treatment RMSPE.

### Findings relevant to this project

- Rwanda's GDP fell sharply in 1994 relative to its synthetic counterfactual.
- The estimated GDP gap closed after approximately 17 years.
- Industry and services experienced larger and more persistent losses than agriculture.
- The disproportionate loss of educated and urban residents motivates studying human-capital recovery separately from GDP recovery.
