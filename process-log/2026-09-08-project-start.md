# Project start - 2026-09-08

## Direction

The proposed project will replicate Hodler's synthetic-control analysis of Rwanda's post-genocide GDP recovery and extend the approach to human-capital outcomes.

## Work completed

- Identified Hodler (2018) as the central replication study.
- Drafted a preliminary research question, knowledge gap, and stakeholder argument.
- Identified secondary-school enrollment, the Penn World Table human-capital index, and Barro-Lee average years of schooling as candidate outcomes.
- Recognized limited pre-treatment observations in Barro-Lee as a feasibility risk.
- Established a weekly capstone work schedule and assignment reminders.

## Open decisions

- Is replication plus one primary extension the appropriate scope?
- Is secondary-school enrollment a defensible primary measure of human-capital recovery?
- Should the replication use the original data vintage before any updated specification is attempted?
- Which donor-pool exclusions and predictors should carry over to the extension?
- What minimum analysis should be completed for the midterm deliverable?

## Next actions

1. Obtain advisor feedback on scope and outcome selection.
2. Audit candidate datasets for years, missingness, definitions, and donor-country coverage.
3. Document Hodler's exact specification and country weights.
4. Begin the replication only after recording sources and licensing constraints.
